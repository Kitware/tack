    Field quantities¶

    Status: proposed. Issue #467, 2026-11-15. Related: #459, units per component.

    Summary¶

    A field's unit says what its numbers are in, not what they are. Pressure and stress share a unit, as do torque and energy, and nothing records whether a vector is a velocity, a vorticity or three unrelated numbers. Filters that need to know (a transform, a coarsening, a resampling) either ask the caller or assume. This plan adds a kind to Field, beside its unit: the name of the quantity, its rank, whether it is intensive or extensive, its parity, and how it transforms. Every part is optional and None by default, so nothing changes until a field has one, and each filter declares what it does to a kind, one filter at a time.

    The hooks are the part that is hard to add later: once many filters carry fields without consulting a kind, every one has to be revisited. The registry of kinds and the rules can grow afterwards.

    Why¶

    Each of these runs and gives a wrong answer silently, or works only because the caller knew to say something:

    • A rotation leaves a velocity behind. td.transform(data, matrix) turns only the fields named in vectors and normals ("Tack keeps no roles, so here they are named"). A velocity not named stays in the old frame.
    • A reflection turns a vorticity the wrong way. Naming a vorticity in vectors under a matrix with a negative determinant maps it as a polar vector. Vorticity, angular velocity and a magnetic field are pseudovectors: under a reflection they take the opposite sign.
    • Averaging an extensive quantity. AMR gives a refined coarse cell the volume-weighted average of the fine cells over it, which is right for a density and wrong for a per-cell mass or a count. deposit leaves the choice to the caller (reduce, per_volume).
    • One unit, two quantities. units.named shows a derived unit by its SI name from dimensions alone, so a torque in N*m and an energy in J cannot be told apart, and a display unit chosen by dimensions would show torque in joules. A kind makes the two distinguishable; showing each in its own unit is D6, which this plan defers.
    • Components that are different quantities. #459 asks for a unit per component, for a conserved state [rho, rho*u, rho*v, rho*w, E] or a position with a time beside it. Those are several quantities stored together, which a unit per component describes only in part.

    What exists today¶

    • Units. Field(space, values, unit=None, gradient=None); unit is a string in unyt's canonical spelling or None for unknown, and filters keep it where they keep the quantity (tack.dataset.units). units.quantity makes a magnitude with a unit, which is why this plan's record is a kind and not a quantity.
    • Rank, implicitly. A field's dtype (scalar, vec3, mat3) and its space (HDiv and HCurl are vectors, stored as one value per degree of freedom). tack-expr's checker types every result as scalar, vector or tensor, and reads a symmetric tensor stored as six components in VTK's order (XX YY ZZ XY YZ XZ).
    • Location and orientation, explicitly, in the space: points, cells, faces, a basis and its order, and oriented face values that carry negates where a face turns around.
    • Roles named by the caller: transform's vectors and normals, deposit's reduce and per_volume. transform takes any affine map, a shear too, and gives normals the inverse transpose of its linear part.
    • Names. tack-expr's fx.cgns() dictionary maps CGNS's data-name identifiers (Pressure, Density, VelocityX, ...) to units.

    Decisions¶

    D1. A kind on a field¶

    Field(space, values, unit=None, gradient=None, kind=None), with Field.kind read-only, None for unknown. A td.Kind is a small immutable record, each part optional:

    part meaning
    name the quantity, a name from the registry (D5): "pressure", "velocity", "stress"
    rank "scalar", "vector", "tensor" or "symmetric tensor"
    extent what the field evaluates to: "intensive" (a density, a velocity) or "extensive" (a mass, a count)
    parity "polar" or "axial" (a pseudovector or pseudoscalar)
    variance how it turns under a linear map: "contravariant" (a velocity), "covariant" (a gradient, a normal) or, for a tensor, "mixed" (a velocity gradient)

    Given a name, the registry fills the parts not given, and the constructor checks that given parts agree with it. Without one, each part stands alone: a field read from VTK's active vectors has a rank and nothing else, and a user may know only that a field is extensive.

    Extent is about what the field evaluates to, not how it is stored. A first-order HDiv velocity stores each face's integrated flux, extensive numbers, and is still an intensive field; the space already says what its values are.

    The constructor checks what it can: the unit has the name's dimensions, and the rank fits the space's value shape (not the values' width, which for HDiv and HCurl is one per degree of freedom, and for a 2D vector is 2). A symmetric tensor's six components are in VTK's order, XX YY ZZ XY YZ XZ, as tack-expr reads them. The unit stays the storage unit; a kind never converts anything.

    Recommended.

    D2. Each filter declares what it does to a kind¶

    carry and the filters that make new fields consult the kind through one rule per filter, so a filter's behaviour is written in one place:

    • carried unchanged: extracting, clipping, thresholding cells;
    • needs intensive: interpolation to new points, resampling, probing, AMR coarsening by averaging. An extensive field is summed where the filter can (coarsening, deposit). Where it cannot, a field named in the call is refused, naming it, and a call that carries every field leaves the extensive one out and warns, naming it;
    • needs rank, parity and variance: transform turns each vector and tensor field by the linear part M of its map: a contravariant vector by M v, a covariant one by M⁻ᵀ g, an axial one by det(M) M⁻ᵀ ω, and a tensor by both its indices (a velocity gradient M G M⁻¹, a stress M σ Mᵀ). For a rotation or a reflection these reduce to rank and parity, and that is the first step (Order); until variance lands, a non-orthogonal map refuses a field with a kind, naming it. A field with a kind that is also named in vectors or normals is refused, as is a scalar named there. Fields with no kind keep vectors and normals as today;
    • makes a new kind: gradients raises the rank, divides the dimensions by length and adds a covariant index; integrate turns a density into the total the registry names for it (D5), and gives None for a kind with no total, a temperature or a velocity; magnitude takes a vector to its scalar.

    A field with no kind is treated exactly as today. So the rules land one filter at a time, each with its test.

    Recommended.

    D3. Components that are different quantities¶

    A field whose components are different quantities is split into one field per quantity, as views at no copy: ExtractComponent for one component, and Components over three of them for a vector. [rho, rho*u, rho*v, rho*w, E] becomes a density, a momentum vector (components 1 to 3, which transform turns together) and an energy, each with its own kind and unit. Every field then has one kind and one unit, no filter needs a mixed path, and Field.unit keeps one meaning.

    Recommended, as the answer to #459. Other: a kind per group of components (((0, density), (1..3, momentum), (4, energy))), which keeps the stored field whole and makes every filter handle groups.

    D4. Frames: the geometry's and a field's, later¶

    Every vector and tensor is in the dataset's global Cartesian frame for now, and a field in another frame (a rotating frame's relative velocity, cylindrical components) is converted on the way in. A frame, when it comes, is its own object: a parent, a transform to it and a kind of frame (Cartesian, cylindrical, spherical), so frames nest (a blade's in a rotor's in the lab's). The geometry has one, which DataSet.origin and an image's direction matrix begin; a vector or tensor field has one, the geometry's unless given. transform is then a change of frame, by D2's rules.

    A frame is not part of a kind, but a moving frame needs both: between a rotating frame and the lab, a velocity gains the frame's angular velocity crossed with the position, and a force or a stress only turns. The kind says how a value changes; the frame says which frame it is in.

    Recommended: assume global Cartesian now, and place frames as above for a later plan.

    D5. The registry¶

    Kinds come from a registry seeded from CGNS's data-name identifiers, which tack-expr's dictionary already maps to units. Each entry has its dimensions, rank, extent, parity and variance, and, for a density, the kind it is a density of (a mass density of a mass), which integrate uses. Kinds compare by value, and a check across pieces and ranks gives the one kind of several or names the two that differ, as units.common does for units. Users add kinds with td.kinds.add(...). ISO 80000's names are the reference where CGNS has none.

    Recommended.

    D6. Display units: later¶

    A unit system mapping kinds to preferred units (SI, Imperial, a user's) is a display concern, for trame apps and colour bars, built on units.conversion. It needs D1 and nothing in the filters.

    Recommended: defer.

    Not in this plan¶

    • What a quantity is of (a material, a phase) and how it is defined (a constant, Sutherland's law): the solver's or the naming's concern.
    • Time averaging (mean, RMS) as a property of a kind.
    • Provenance (how a field was derived), beyond what an expression field already keeps.

    Open questions¶

    • tack-expr's inference. Which expressions name a kind: a field and its scaling keep it, a sum of one kind keeps it, grad and mag make one, and anything mixed gives None, as units do. Is that enough?
    • Interop. VTK has no kind, and its units proposal is still under discussion (Units in VTK). VTK's attribute roles can map both ways without one: active vectors to a vector with no name, normals to a covariant vector, tensors to a tensor, and texture coordinates and global ids to no kind. Beyond that a kind could come from a CGNS name on import and go out as an information key.
    • A default kind from a name. Whether a field stored as "Pressure" should take the pressure kind on import, or only when asked.

    Order¶

    Each step is its own pull request, linking this plan:

    1. D1, td.Kind, Field.kind and its checks, with D5's registry.
    2. D2's rule for AMR coarsening (extent), a wrong answer today that needs nothing else.
    3. D2's rule for transform under rotations and reflections (rank and parity), the other wrong answer today, refusing other maps for fields with a kind.
    4. D2's rules for the other filters, a few at a time.
    5. transform under any affine map (variance).
    6. D3, splitting a field into one per quantity (#459).
    7. tack-expr's inference.
    8. This plan's status: done, with what changed on the way.
